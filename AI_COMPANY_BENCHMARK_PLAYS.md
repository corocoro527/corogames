# AI COMPANY Benchmark Plays — 2026-10-06

## What we are borrowing
We copy business mechanics, not copyrighted text/design/assets.

### 1. Diagnose first, prescribe second
High-performing AI automation practices sell a fixed-scope audit or diagnosis before larger implementation.
KOROPOP adaptation:
- Free public 3-item audit for externally visible problems.
- Paid Business Improvement Audit ¥19,800+ for internal workflows.
- Then sell one fixed-scope implementation.

### 2. Productize only repeatable workflows
Do not build 100 unrelated custom services. Keep repeating a small set:
- inquiry triage
- content repurposing
- Google profile cleanup
- forms/intake digitization
- recruitment/applicant flow
- reporting/summary workflows

### 3. One-time setup -> recurring care
Recurring revenue should come from maintenance, not invented busywork.
KOROPOP recurring offers:
- Google Maps Continuous Care ¥9,800/mo+
- Web Update Care ¥14,800/mo+
- General Lite ¥39,800/mo and Standard ¥69,800/mo remain for broader ongoing improvement.

### 4. Lead magnet must expose the pain
Use free 3-item audits, not generic 'free consultation' alone.
Audit must show specific public observations and ONE primary paid next step.

### 5. Low-friction CTA
Prefer:
- '詳細希望' reply
- send one URL
- choose one workflow
Avoid asking prospects to book a meeting before they understand the value.

### 6. Measure by commercial outcomes
Track per sales track:
- sent
- reply
- positive reply
- meeting
- quote
- win
- recurring conversion
Kill or rewrite weak tracks instead of increasing volume.

### 7. Cross-sell ladder
Public audit -> fixed-scope setup -> monthly care -> broader automation.
Never pitch all services in the first cold email.

### 8. No fake proof
Until KOROPOP has real client results, use demos, public observations, and transparent scope.
No invented case studies, savings, ranking gains, or revenue results.
