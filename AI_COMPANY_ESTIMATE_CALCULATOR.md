## Prospect signals
- price depends on area / size / count / plan / options
- website says "料金はお問い合わせ" but public service conditions are fairly structured
- repeated quote requests likely happen before real consultation
- service business has multiple add-ons or plan combinations
- existing website already gets leads but forms do not pre-qualify them

## Good targets
Cleaning, painting, moving, repair, auto services, photography/video, rentals, lessons, selected home services.

## Sales angle
Do not claim conversion lift.
Say:
"相談前に『だいたいいくらか』を確認できると、問い合わせ時点で希望条件を具体化できます。"

## Safety / scope
- Use estimate/range when final price depends on inspection.
- Never present an estimate as contractually final unless client explicitly designs it that way and legal/operational review is complete.
- No paid calculator SaaS required by default.
- Do not collect unnecessary personal data.
- SMS/paid integrations require approval.
