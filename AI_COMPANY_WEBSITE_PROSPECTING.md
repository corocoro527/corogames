# Website Production & Prospecting Playbook

## Offer
小規模ホームページ制作・リニューアル
- MINI ¥79,800+ / up to 3 pages
- STANDARD ¥129,800+ / up to 5 pages
- GROWTH ¥198,000+ / around 7 pages, scope confirmed first

## Best prospect signals
- official website does not exist but business is active and contactable
- only Instagram / Google Maps / directory listing is used
- existing site is not mobile-friendly or CTA is unclear
- company/service information is scattered across old pages
- site still works, but contact/recruit/service paths are difficult to find
- active hiring or active service promotion with weak owned website

## Sales rule
Do NOT say 'your site is old/bad'.
Use:
- '公開情報を拝見し、初めて見る方がサービス内容へ進みやすくできる箇所がありました'
- '全面リニューアルではなく、既存内容を活かす方が合う可能性があります'

## First offer
Free 3-item website audit:
1. what the business does
2. mobile/CTA path
3. trust/contact information

Only propose a rebuild when public evidence suggests a rebuild is justified.
If small fixes are enough, sell inquiry-flow / web-care instead.

## Cross-sell ladder
Website -> Google Maps cleanup -> inquiry automation -> Web Update Care.
Do not pitch all at once.

## Scope boundaries
No ranking/revenue guarantees.
No rights-unclear copied text/images.
No domain/server/paid-plugin purchases without approval.
Complex EC/member/reservation core systems are separate scope.
