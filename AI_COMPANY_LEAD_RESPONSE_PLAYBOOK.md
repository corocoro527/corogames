# Lead Response Starter — Sales & Delivery Rules

## Offer
問い合わせ即時フォロー・取りこぼし防止スターター：49,800円〜

## Good fit
- Webフォームや公開メールで見積・予約・相談が入る
- 小規模チームで担当振り分けが手作業
- 問い合わせ後の受付・社内通知・対応状況の管理を整理したい
- existing Gmail/Google Sheets can cover the first implementation

## Base workflow
Inquiry received -> acknowledgement -> internal structured notification -> tracking sheet/status -> unresolved check.

## Safety / scope
- No autonomous pricing, contracting, legal/medical judgment, or sales negotiation.
- No SMS/phone automation unless provider, consent, cost, and operational rules are separately confirmed.
- Do not buy or sign up for tools without approval.
- Never claim faster response/revenue improvement as a result unless actually measured.
- Personal data scope must be minimized and agreed before implementation.

## Sales positioning
Sell the operational gap after the inquiry, not generic AI.
Do not say the company is slow or losing leads unless verified.
Use phrasing like: 「問い合わせ後の受付・社内共有・対応状況を1つの流れにできます」.
